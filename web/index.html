Image Resize

Shrink a whole batch of images in one go. Pick a maximum size, and every image is scaled down to fit, keeping its shape. Perfect before uploading to a website, email or form.

Everything happens in your browser. Your images are never uploaded.

1 Choose your images

Drag images or a folder here, or

JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP and AVIF. HEIC (iPhone) works in Safari.

2 Choose the size

Images are only ever made smaller, never larger. Location and camera details are removed.

3 Resize and save

Choose some images to get started.
